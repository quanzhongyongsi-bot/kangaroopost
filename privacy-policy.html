Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kangaroo Post ("we", "us", or "the Operator") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Kangaroo Post service (the "Service"), a scheduling and publishing service that helps creators publish their own videos to their own TikTok accounts.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 TikTok account information. When you connect your TikTok account to the Service through TikTok's official authorization (OAuth) flow, we receive and store:

- Your TikTok user identifier (open ID), username (nickname), and profile image URL;

- An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, which allow the Service to publish content to your account on your behalf, only as described in this Policy.

We never ask for, receive, or store your TikTok password.

1.2 Content you provide. We store the content you upload or create in the Service in order to publish it as scheduled, including:

- Video files you upload for posting;

- Captions, hashtags, and post settings you enter (such as scheduled time, privacy level, interaction settings, and commercial content disclosure settings);

- Records of your scheduled and published posts and their publishing status.

1.3 Usage and technical information. We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the Service, such as access logs, timestamps of operations, and error records.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only for the following purposes:

- To publish, at the time you scheduled, the videos and captions you reviewed and approved, to the TikTok account you connected;

- To display your connected account (nickname and profile image) in the Service so you can confirm where a post will be published;

- To show you the status of your scheduled and published posts;

- To maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Service, and to respond to your inquiries.

We do not use your information for advertising profiling, and we do not publish anything to your account that you have not uploaded, reviewed, and approved in advance.

3. Sharing of Information

- We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

- Your videos, captions, and post settings are transmitted to TikTok through TikTok's official APIs in order to publish your posts. TikTok's handling of that information is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

- We may use third-party service providers (such as cloud hosting providers) to operate the Service. Such providers process information only as necessary to provide their services to us and are required to protect it appropriately.

- We may disclose information if required by law or by a competent authority.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

- Your content and post records are retained while your account with the Service is active, or as needed to provide the Service.

- If you disconnect your TikTok account from the Service, we revoke and delete the stored access tokens, and the Service can no longer publish to your account.

- If you request deletion of your account or your information, we will delete your stored personal information, uploaded content, and tokens within 30 days of the request, except for information we are required by law to retain.

- You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time using the contact information below.

5. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information, including access controls and encrypted storage of authorization tokens. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for creators who are 18 years of age or older (or the age required to hold a TikTok account and enter into agreements in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with the "Last updated" date revised. Significant changes will be notified through the Service where appropriate.
